Undergraduate Thesis

LIBS Spectroscopy & Cold Plasma Analysis

NATIONAL POLYTECHNIC SCHOOL · LIBS LABORATORY

Design and construction of an experimental device for Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy (LIBS) based on plasmas generated by high-voltage discharges. The work covers the physical analysis of the cold plasma produced, the study of its thermodynamic properties, and the application of computational models to spectral data.

Estimation of plasma electron temperature and density using the Saha-Boltzmann equation, combining experimental data with computational methods to characterize the thermodynamic state of the plasma.

Experiments involved synthesis and handling of chemical samples under strict safety protocols, spectroscopic analysis with data from over 1,000 samples, and the application of ensemble Machine Learning models (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting) for quantitative estimation of elemental concentrations from spectral signals.
