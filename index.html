Principal research

Undergraduate thesis

LIBS Spectroscopy & Cold Plasma Analysis

National Polytechnic School · LIBS Laboratory

Design and construction of an experimental device for Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy (LIBS) based on plasmas generated by high-voltage discharges — covering the physical characterisation of the cold plasma, its thermodynamic properties, and the application of computational models to spectral data.

Estimating plasma electron temperature and density via the Saha–Boltzmann equation, combining experimental measurements with computational methods to characterise the thermodynamic state of the plasma.

The work spanned sample synthesis and handling under strict safety protocols, spectroscopic analysis across more than 1,000 samples, and ensemble machine-learning models (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting) for quantitative estimation of elemental concentrations from spectral signals.
